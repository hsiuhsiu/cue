The story

Why I made Cue.

A familiar habit.
A tool of my own.

I went from Quicksilver to Alfred, then to Raycast. These tools kept adding features and getting more complex, but I kept coming back to the same handful of things.

I care about privacy, too. So I figured: I know what I need—why not make my own, get those features right, and make it as fast as I can? With AI helping out, building it myself didn’t cost nearly as much time and effort. That’s how Cue happened.

The source is open as well. Use it as it is, or fork it, change it, and build your own. Add what you’re missing, take out what you don’t need, and make a Cue that feels right for you.

Your Mac, your choice.

Local tools work offline. You decide whether Cue can connect for updates, GPT, or exchange rates. Browser searches happen only when you send them. Window controls and selected-text replacement need macOS Accessibility permission.
