Cue modifications: the dictionaries' first/default output candidates and the
s2twp/tw2sp configuration plans are compiled into a compact Unicode-scalar trie
format. Cue's Swift implementation follows OpenCC's normalization, maximum-match
segmentation, ordered dictionary-group conversion, and ideographic-description
sequence handling. OpenCC itself is not linked or installed at runtime.

Reproduction instructions and exact input/output hashes are distributed with
Cue's source in docs/chinese-conversion-data.md and
scripts/chinese-conversion-data.json.
